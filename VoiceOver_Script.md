## How to record with the deck

1. Open `index.html` in full screen (F11).
2. The teleprompter at the bottom shows the line for the current slide. The top bar tracks the 5:00 timeline.
3. Turn AUTO on (button or press `A`) so slides advance themselves on the timeline, then press Space to start and read along.
4. Watch the "slide left" counter turn red at five seconds to know when to land the line.
5. For a clean recording with no controls, press `H` to hide all presenter tools, or press the `×` on the teleprompter (or `T`) to hide just the script.
6. Manual control: arrow keys change slides, Space plays or pauses, `R` resets, `T` toggles the script.

### [0:00 to 0:12] SLIDE 1, TITLE
> ShieldGuard. Vishing detection using machine learning and natural language processing. An International EUREKA Innovation Exhibition 2026 entry by Mohamad Ikmal Hafizi of UniKL MIIT.

*Direction: calm, confident open. Let the logo animation breathe.*

### [0:12 to 0:42] SLIDE 2, THE CHALLENGE
> Every day, Malaysians lose fortunes to a voice on the phone. In 2025, online fraud took RM2.97 billion from victims, and telecommunication scams alone accounted for over RM800 million. These are not random calls. They are scripted psychological attacks. A fake officer. A fake bank. Urgency, fear, and a demand for your one time password.

*Direction: serious, urgent. The counters animate as you say the figures.*

### [0:42 to 1:05] SLIDE 3, WHY DEFENCES FAIL
> Today's defences chase phone numbers, using blocklists and caller ID. But scammers switch to a new number in seconds, so the block never catches up. The real danger was never the number. It is the words being spoken, and nobody was reading the conversation. Until now.

*Direction: build tension toward "until now".*

### [1:05 to 1:30] SLIDE 4, SOLUTION AND ABSTRACT
> Meet ShieldGuard. A detector that listens to what is actually said on a call. It turns the call into text, studies the language with a hybrid AI engine, and tells you in plain words why a call is dangerous and what to do next. Not after the fraud. During it.

*Direction: shift to hopeful, solution energy.*

### [1:30 to 1:52] SLIDE 5, OBJECTIVES
> ShieldGuard has three goals. First, spot vishing from the conversation itself, as the call happens, using machine learning and language analysis. Second, explain every verdict with explainable AI, so users trust it and act. Third, verify numbers and accounts against live threat intelligence to confirm the risk.

*Direction: crisp, one beat per objective as each card reveals.*

### [1:52 to 2:32] SLIDE 6, HOW IT WORKS
> Here is how it works. A hybrid engine with three layers. First, the call audio becomes text with Whisper. Layer one is a machine learning model that reads the wording and owns the risk score. Layer two searches a memory of real scam scripts for anything that matches. Layer three is a large language model that reviews the case and explains the tactics in plain words. And this is the key point. Machine learning stays in charge. The AI explains the result. It never overrides strong evidence.

*Direction: steady, technical authority. Gesture across the four nodes.*

### [2:32 to 3:32] SLIDE 7, LIVE DEMO
> Let us see it live. A suspicious call comes in. The user pastes the transcript, uploads a recording, or captures it straight from the browser, and ShieldGuard turns it into text in an instant. The risk gauge climbs into the red as the model detects pressure, urgency, and a request for a one time password. The explainable AI panel highlights the exact scam phrases, names the tactic, impersonation and fear, and gives clear next steps. Hang up. Never share your password. Call the bank directly. Behind the scenes, the system checks the number against known scam databases, and every detection is logged to a secure dashboard. From spoken words to a clear verdict, in seconds.

*Direction: this is the money slide. Sync narration to the on screen demo video. The 60 seconds matches the visible actions.*

### [3:32 to 3:54] SLIDE 8, PERFORMANCE
> And it performs. On a clean, separate test set, ShieldGuard reaches 98.88 percent accuracy, 99.2 percent balanced accuracy, and a 98.69 percent macro F1 score. It correctly flagged 245 of 249 scam calls, and every single safe call. Proven with repeated cross validation, not a lucky split.

*Direction: proud, factual. Numbers land hard.*

### [3:54 to 4:16] SLIDE 9, NOVELTY
> What makes it original? Everyone else blocks numbers. ShieldGuard reads the conversation. It is the first to blend a machine learning engine, a memory of real scams, and an AI reviewer, built so the AI can never invent a story over hard evidence. Transparent, trustworthy, and hard to fool.

*Direction: emphasise "first" and "hard to fool".*

### [4:16 to 4:38] SLIDE 10, MARKETABILITY
> The market is real and urgent. ShieldGuard ships as an interface for banks, telcos, and call centres, and as a mobile app for everyday users, free to start, over a fraud problem worth billions of ringgit. One engine, three ways to earn, and a clear path from campus prototype to product.

*Direction: business confidence.*

### [4:38 to 4:56] SLIDE 11, IMPACT AND SDG
> The impact is human and global. ShieldGuard protects families, supports fraud teams, and advances United Nations Goals 8, 9, and 16. Safer economies, stronger infrastructure, and better institutions. And it is fully digital, needs no hardware, and is green by design.

*Direction: warm, values forward. Ties to the theme.*

### [4:56 to 5:00] SLIDE 12, CLOSING
> ShieldGuard. Detect the words. Explain the risk. Stop the scam.

*Direction: slow, deliberate, three beat close. End on the tagline.*
